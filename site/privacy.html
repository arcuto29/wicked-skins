Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Priism Skins (the browser extension, the desktop app and this website) is a cosmetic tool for TradingView charts. This page explains what data it handles.

On your computer only

  • Your settings (wallpaper, candle colours, adjustments, saved looks) are stored in your browser or the desktop app.
  • Your own images and videos are stored on your computer. Videos the browser can't play are converted on your computer; they are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Chart pages: the extension draws behind and on the chart. It does not read or send your orders, positions, balance, account details or browsing history. The “Live mood” features read the price in the tab title and the P&L text already drawn on your own chart, on your computer only; nothing is sent anywhere.

Only if you use the Workshop

  • Browsing and using Workshop wallpapers needs no account — in the extension, the desktop app or on this website. Your browser simply downloads the wallpapers you pick. The extension never asks you to sign in.
  • Signing in to share your own happens on this website (Google or email + password). It stores your email address and a display name with our backend provider, Supabase, so you can share. With Google sign-in we receive your name and email from Google; nothing else.
  • Wallpapers you share are stored with Supabase and become public after review.
  • You can delete your shared wallpapers at any time. To delete your account and data, email .

Updates

The desktop app and zip installs check GitHub Releases for new versions. This sends a normal web request, without personal data.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of data. Data is used only to run the features above.
