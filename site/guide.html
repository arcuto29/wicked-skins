Getting started

Install it, open a TradingView chart, pick a look. It's purely visual — it never reads your orders, positions or account.

  1. Install. Click Add to Chrome (works in Edge, Brave, Opera and Arc too) and confirm Add extension.
  2. Pin it (optional). Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Priism Skins, so its icon is always there.
  3. Open a chart. Go to tradingview.com and open any chart.
  4. Pick a look. Click the Priism Skins icon → the panel opens → tap a One-click look (Neon Night, Blackout, Cosmic…). Your wallpaper and candle colours apply instantly.
  5. Tweak it. Use the tabs — Skins, Style (candles, glow, price scale), Uploads (your own images/videos), Adjust (darken, blur, Sides, Interface).

Tip: Alt+Shift+W on the chart turns everything on or off instantly.

  1. Download the app for your system from the Download section — Windows (.exe), macOS or Linux (.AppImage).
  2. Install & open it. On first launch it opens to the Skins & settings window.
  3. Open your chart. Click Open TradingView with skins. TradingView opens in its own window with your skin already applied.
  4. Choose a look in the settings window; it updates the chart live. Your settings are saved on your computer.
  5. Updates install themselves — you'll be asked to restart when a new version is ready.

Windows may show a “Windows protected your PC” box on first run — click More info → Run anyway. On macOS, if it says it can't verify the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Beta

  1. Download the APK from the Download section (the Android card).
  2. Allow the install. Android will ask to allow installing from your browser/files — approve it, then tap the APK to install.
  3. Open the app. It loads TradingView with Priism Skins built in.
  4. Tap the settings button (bottom-right) to pick skins and candle colours.

TradingView's own app can't run add-ons, which is why this is a separate app. A Play Store version is coming.

  1. Open your platform's chart (e.g. TopstepX, or any site that uses TradingView charts) in Chrome.
  2. Click the Priism Skins icon. If it finds a chart, you'll see “Chart found on …” → Turn on here.
  3. Approve once. Chrome asks to allow the extension on that site — that's its standard wording for drawing on a page. The page reloads skinned.
  4. Turn off in the same place whenever you want; it removes the skin and the permission.

Some prop firms have rules about browser extensions on funded or evaluation accounts — check yours first. Priism Skins only changes how the chart looks and never touches your orders.

Sharing a wallpaper (Workshop)

  1. Browse & use community wallpapers in the extension's Workshop tab — no account needed. Tap Use and it's on your chart.
  2. Share your own on the Workshop page: sign in, drag in an image or video, add a title, and submit. Uploads are reviewed before they appear.

Quick fixes

I don't see any change on my chart

Make sure the panel's top switch is On, and that you're on an actual chart page (tradingview.com/chart). Try Alt+Shift+W.

The candles are hard to see on a busy wallpaper

In Adjust → Picture, raise Darken, or lower the wallpaper's Opacity. Or turn on Match wallpaper (Style → Candles) so the candles take colours that stand out.

The price scale text is hard to read

It auto-adjusts, but you can set it manually in Style → Price scale (text colour, glass backing, size).

Two copies installed

If you loaded it unpacked and also installed from the store, remove one at chrome://extensions so they don't both draw.

Does it slow things down?

It pauses in background tabs and has an Eco mode and Auto quality that lower detail if your computer struggles. Set it in Adjust → Picture.
