Zone shape

Applies to every zone and every rectangle you draw. Pill rounds the ends of wide boxes and gives tall boxes rounded corners. Price in a zone lights up a zone, or a rectangle you drew, while price is inside it: Glow and Strong brighten it with a soft pulse, Edge lights only its border, Spotlight puts a soft light right at the price inside it, and Shimmer slides a slow sheen across it. The candles always stay clear. To set one zone on its own, use Each indicator below.

Proximity glow lights the nearest zone edge as price gets close. Contact ripple sends one soft ripple along the edge price first touches. Liquid border runs a light round the zone price is in or near. Directional light lights the edge price came in through. Zone fusion tints the live candle's glow with the zone's colour. They follow the live candle, so they show when the newest candle is on screen.

Focus hides and pauses the wallpaper so only your chart is left. Press it again to bring it back. "Everything" also removes the chart styling. Your P&L, goal bar, timer and brackets stay. The clip shortcut records for the length set in Capture; press it again to stop early.

Magnifier

A round zoom lens that follows your cursor on the chart, for exact wicks, levels and entries without changing the chart's zoom. Turn it on and off with its shortcut (above), or press Esc. Clicks go straight through it.
